Interface direction · WP-15b

Fresh food deserves an interface that doesn't look expired

The current shell is six emoji, a stranded heading and a void. Here is the diagnosis, and a system that replaces it: quiet by default, expressive exactly where the food is about to go off. Everything below is live — change the accent and the theme and watch the whole page follow.

What the screenshot is actually telling us

Six problems. Five are visual; one is a bug that only looks visual.

Bug

The desktop nav isn't a nav, it's a footer

There is a min-width: 768px rule setting the tab bar to position: static — but the nav is rendered after <main>. Dropping out of fixed positioning lands it below a full-height main, so it reads as a stuck mobile tab bar. The fix is DOM order, not CSS.

Identity

The icons are emoji strings

🏠 📖 🥫 📅 🛒 ⚙️ sit in NAV_ITEMS as text. They can't inherit currentColor, can't animate, can't take a weight, and render as a different artist's work on every OS. This one choice does most of the damage.

Surface

One flat plane, no elevation

Background, content and nav share a single near-black. Nothing is a card, nothing is raised, so the eye has no structure to hold — the page reads as a void with text in the corner.

Layout

Content is stranded, not composed

The heading sits hard against the top-left with no container, no measure, no rhythm. On a wide viewport that leaves roughly nine-tenths of the screen empty and unexplained.

Copy

Developer text is leaking into the product

“Stub route — see WP-23.” is a note to us, shown to the user. Every route needs a real empty state that says what to do next.

State

Status is text, not affordance

“No workbook · Signed out” are two dead labels where the two most important actions in the app should be. Nothing looks pressable.

The proposal, running

Tap a row. Switch the accent. These are the real interactions, not pictures of them.

The system underneath

Three rules do most of the work. The rest is spacing discipline.

Colour

Accent
Hue only, OKLCH, L fixed per role
Neutrals
Green-biased, never pure grey
Surfaces
3 levels: paper → surface → raised
Semantic
Fixed hues, independent of accent

Motion

Micro
160 ms — hover, press
State
220 ms — check, toggle
Entry
260–300 ms — tab, draw
Easing
cubic-bezier(.22,.61,.36,1)
Never
Layout or position animation

Type

Display
Fraunces — headings only
UI
Archivo — everything else
Quantities
tabular-nums, always
Measure
~65 characters
One rule earns its place above the others: only exceptions get colour. Fresh items are neutral; amber means expiring; red means expired. That keeps a pantry of thirty items calm instead of a fruit salad — and it sidesteps a real collision, because the accent is user-selectable. If “fresh” were accent-green and someone picked amber, the expiry warning would vanish into the interface. Semantic colour must never derive from the accent hue.
