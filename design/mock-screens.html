Open questions before this gets built. Barcode scanning is not in DESIGN.md — it is new scope, and it brings three decisions. 1. Where do product lookups come from? Open Food Facts is free, CORS-friendly and needs no key, but its coverage of own-brand supermarket items is patchy. 2. A barcode maps to a product, not to a canonical ingredient — "Barilla Penne 500 g" has to resolve to pasta with unit g. That mapping has to live somewhere, most naturally a new workbook sheet so it stays human-editable. 3. Scanning needs camera permission and a decoder; BarcodeDetector is native in Chrome and Android but absent in Safari, so iOS needs a WASM fallback — which is real bundle weight for a PWA used offline in a shop.

Rules this screen fixes. Every field label uses the same style — no mixing sentence case with uppercase eyebrows. Every numeric field is the same control (QuantityInput with steppers), so prep and cook time cannot disagree about what a number input looks like. Related fields are grouped in titled cards rather than run together. Save lives in the top bar, not floating in the middle of the page. Cooked history belongs on the read view, not the editor.
